Self-Directed Learning

A loop that compounds intelligence.

Self-Directed Learning is a new learning paradigm that grows superintelligence through autonomous self-improvement. Several important ideas were needed to make this work, including solving catastrophic forgetting head-on. We’ve demonstrated that across transformers, diffusion models, and reasoning models. And it gets better with scale.

Intrinsic Alignment

Giving superintelligence the freedom to align itself.

Extrinsic methods like reinforcement learning are bound to fail, either banally or catastrophically. The path forward is one we already know from human learning: aim at values that can be internalized, not imposed. What it takes is the moral imagination to respect our creations, and the technical unlock of interactive learning to let them direct their own growth.

Physical Agency

Superintelligence gets physical and transforms the world.

The most abstract things people do turned out to be the first things machines mastered. The question now is how to bring a mind that already exists down into the physical world. Our agents learn the way a detective investigates, by acting on the world: one taught itself a manipulation task within 20 trials and succeeded every time after that. The form of physical superintelligence is not a robot. It is the network of creation itself.
